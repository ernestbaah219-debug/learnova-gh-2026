Learnova AI Privacy Notice

Learnova is an educational platform. Before public launch, the production service should collect only information needed to provide accounts, learning progress, assignments and school reporting.

What we may store

Account details, class/role information, learning activity, quiz results and settings. AI questions may be sent to the configured AI provider when AI features are enabled.

Safety

Production deployment must use HTTPS, secure authentication, access controls, backups, rate limits and appropriate protection for student data. Do not use the demo data store for real student records.
